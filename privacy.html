Privacy policy

Gareth Personal Gmail CLI is a private tool used only by Gareth Green on his own Google account.

What it accesses

With the account owner's permission it uses two Gmail permissions: managing Gmail settings (filters) and managing labels. It does not read, send or delete email.

What it does with data

Filter and label settings are read and changed only on the owner's own account, at the owner's request. No data is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising. Sign-in tokens are stored encrypted on the owner's own computer.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time from the Google Account page, under Security, Third-party apps and services.
